What a lane is. One (provider, model) pair with its own quota bucket. That distinction is why a $0 deployment has any capacity at all: OVHcloud's anonymous endpoint rate-limits per model, so eleven models are eleven fresh buckets. Measured 2026-11-18: ten rapid calls to one model returned [200,200,200,200,429×6], while ten different models were never limited against each other.

The one rule

An LLM may kill a finding. It may never create one.
A model saying “this looks exploitable” is worth nothing until it executes. A model saying “this is already guarded, the require is on line 91” is worth a lot, because a human verifies that in ten seconds. Asymmetry of evidence, therefore asymmetry of power. Cantina bans unvalidated AI submissions; nothing here writes a report.

How a kill is earned

A finding is dropped only when a quorum of independent lanes returns FALSE_POSITIVE and the stated reason names code a human can go and read. Two gates stand between the panel and the queue:
  • _cites_code() — the reason must contain an identifier, a call, or a guard.
  • _argument_is_substantive() — naming, style and authority claims are vetoed even when they mention code.
Anything else — a lone vote, a tie, a dead panel, a vibe — leaves the finding exactly where it was.

Why the second gate exists

Measured live on GnosisChain tokenbridge-contracts: the panel killed 6 of 9 findings, and while 4 reasons pointed at a real guard, two did not. One argued that “the underscore prefix in _collectGasTokens() indicates internal function, restricting external access, making the unchecked call return value a non-issue.” That is a naming convention, not a mechanism — an internal function can lose funds perfectly well. Two kills in six were pattern-matching to the shape of an argument. Both gates were added the same day those numbers were measured.

What is never done here

No leaked credentials harvested from public repos. No IP or session rotation to dodge a rate limit. No single key driven from many accounts. Those are abuse, they get accounts terminated, and a bounty pipeline that does them is worthless. Rotating across one provider's own models is the provider's published behaviour, and that is all this does.
